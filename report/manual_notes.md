After the first full run I reviewed every failure. The key facts of s05 were widened because "two additional weeks" is a correct answer, and the variant "no" in s15 and m05 was replaced because it also matched "there is no restriction". All 30 questions were re-run for all four systems; first-run results are kept in eval/results\_v1. Because these revisions were made after seeing results on the same set, the reported figures are optimistic compared with a fresh held-out set.
